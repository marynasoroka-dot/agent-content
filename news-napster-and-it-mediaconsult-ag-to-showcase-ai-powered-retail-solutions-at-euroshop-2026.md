# Napster and IT MediaConsult AG to showcase AI-powered retail solutions at EuroShop 2026

The partnership integrates Napster’s advanced conversational AI technology with ITMediaConsult AG’s robust retail ecosystem.

**NEW YORK — February 21, 2026** — **Napster**, an innovation company powering the next generation of embodied and agentic AI, today announced a partnership with **ITMediaConsult AG**, a premier provider of retail IT infrastructure and point-of-sale (POS) solutions. The collaboration will kick off at **EuroShop 2026**, the world’s leading retail trade fair, held in Düsseldorf, Germany, from February 22–26.

The partnership integrates Napster’s advanced conversational AI technology with ITMediaConsult AG’s robust retail ecosystem, which spans POS systems, digital signage, and self-checkout kiosks. Both **Napster Station**, the AI-powered concierge kiosk that will be featured at the ITMediaConsult AG booth at EuroShop, and **Napster Spaces**, a platform that can take any URL and transform it into an AI experience, will be available to ITMediaConsult AG clients. These platforms offer multiple touchpoints for retailers to deploy specialized AI Companions that can assist customers online or directly on the sales floor, in multiple languages, with full visibility on inventory, locations, and whatever else a visitor might need.

ITMediaConsult AG brings over two decades of expertise in custom hardware manufacturing and has managed services for global retail brands, offering valuable learnings as Napster continues to deploy hardware such as Station. Together with Napster, the company is bridging the gap between digital intelligence and physical customer service, providing scalable tools designed to perform in high-traffic, real-world environments.

Attendees at EuroShop 2026 can visit **Hall 5, Booth A29** to experience live demonstrations of Napster-powered AI assistants integrated into the latest retail hardware.
