# Napster view arrives at Best Buy

This holiday season, Napster View lands at Best Buy: a $99 3D holographic display that puts a crew of AI specialists right above your Mac.

This holiday season, **Napster View** lands at Best Buy: a $99 3D holographic display that puts a crew of AI specialists right above your Mac. No glasses. No headset. Just a collaborator who stays present while you work.

Most AI tools still feel like sophisticated search bars. You type, you wait, you read. Napster View is something different: an embodied AI companion who maintains eye contact, responds to what's on your screen, and is always available.

**May** turns half-formed ideas into working prototypes, pitchable demos, or the next piece of content everyone's talking about. In minutes, not weeks.

**Alyssa** helps you nail that job interview, craft standout college applications, and walk into any negotiation with a plan that actually wins.

**Jordan** gets you healthy without the BS. Real food you'll actually eat, workouts you'll actually do, and results by Thursday, not week twelve.

Whenever you need advice, Companions are there for you.
