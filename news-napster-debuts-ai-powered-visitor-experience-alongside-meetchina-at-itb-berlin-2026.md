## *Exhibition Will Showcase How Napster Station Incorporates Third-Party Platforms to Create Personalized Concierge Experiences for Customers*

**BERLIN — March 2, 2026 —** **Napster**, an innovation company powering the next generation of embodied and agentic AI, today announced its participation at **ITB Berlin** alongside **MeetChina** a national inbound tourism consumption demonstration platform under the guidance and support of the Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the People’s Republic of China.

Located in **Hall 26**, the exhibition will showcase how Napster’s AI technology integrates into the MeetChina ecosystem and supports shopping, payments, navigation, and more, all through **Napster Station**hardware powered by **faytech Tech Co, Ltd**.

The activation, facilitated by faytech, showcases how embodied AI hardware can support foreign travelers in China and work with other platforms to create personalized concierge experiences. Napster provides the intelligent software layer and experience orchestration, enabling the station to serve as a:

**Conversational Wayfinder**: Helping visitors navigate complex environments.

**Booking and Shopping Assistant**: Streamlining commerce and reservations.

**Personalized Guide**: Delivering tailored information in multiple languages.

This initiative bridges the gap between digital intelligence and physical customer service. By connecting tourism, commerce, and entertainment, Napster and MeetChina are exhibiting a seamless journey for the next generation of global travelers.

Attendees at ITB Berlin can visit **Hall 26** at the MeetChina booth to experience live demonstrations of the Napster-powered AI concierge.

**About MeetChina**

MeetChina is a national inbound tourism consumption demonstration platform supported and guided by the Department of Industry Development and the Bureau of International Exchange and Cooperation of the Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the People’s Republic of China, and implemented by China Digital Culture Group.

The project officially launched its first pilot in Yuyuan, Shanghai on September 27, 2025, followed by a successful deployment in Silk Street, Beijing on December 29, 2025. It is now expanding across Beijing and Shanghai, as well as into major Chinese cities and emerging “hidden treasure” destinations.

MeetChina’s smart digital stations are designed to increase inbound visitor spending efficiency in key commercial districts by addressing a critical challenge: how to convert arrival into consumption. Through digital infrastructure, the platform enables four core capabilities — instant understanding, instant selection, instant visibility, and instant payment — reducing language barriers and payment friction.

The system serves major inbound visitor groups and supports multiple languages including English, Japanese, Korean, French, German, Spanish, Thai, Russian, and Arabic. It features curated categories such as “What to Eat,” “What to Buy,” and “What to Experience,” integrating time-honored brands and distinctive local merchants.

In collaboration with China UnionPay Merchant Services, international visitors can directly tap overseas bank cards or use Apple Pay, Google Pay, or Samsung Pay on the station to complete payments — enabling seamless foreign card acceptance with contactless transactions under RMB 1,000 requiring no PIN.**‍**
