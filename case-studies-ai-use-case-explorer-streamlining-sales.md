### The Challenge:

**Microsoft's GenAI field sellers were spending time hunting for relevant customer success stories across fragmented PPT decks and SharePoint sites. This scattered approach was slowing down deal preparation.**

### **‍** The Solution: AI Use Case Explorer

A centralized web portal serving as the single source of truth for validated AI design wins, powered by Azure OpenAI and Azure AI Search.**‍**

**Impact Summary**

* Significantly reduced time spent searching for relevant customer success stories
* Achieved widespread adoption across the seller community
* High engagement levels demonstrate value to end users
* Platform supports ongoing content discovery and cross-selling opportunities**‍**

### **‍ Results Achieved**

**Performance Metrics**

* 5x faster asset discovery: From ~2 minutes (PPT search) to 24 seconds (portal)
* 43,585 unique sellers reached in first 12 months
* 80,407 total sessions with frequent return usage
* 237,000+ page views across the platform
* 87% average scroll depth indicating deep engagement
* 2.95 pages per session showing cross-exploration of content

**Key Features**

* **Curated Library:** 100+ validated AI usecases organized by industry and persona
* **Semantic Search:** Natural language queries to find relevant wins
* **Auto-Generated Podcasts:** AI-powered audio briefs for consumption on-the-go
* **Self-Service CMS:** Enables SMEs to add new cases without IT dependency
