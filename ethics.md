# Report unethical behavior.

If you believe a Napster employee is behaving unethically, please follow the process below. All reports are taken seriously and handled with care. This form is intended for reports of ethical misconduct.

Report an Incident

How It Works

Step 1

### Submit your report.

Complete the form with factual details — dates, times, individuals involved, and any supporting
evidence. Do not include personal opinion.

Step 2

### Internal Investigation.

A Napster HR representative will carry out an internal review of the issue.

Step 3

### Follow-up.

If you provided contact details, the HR representative may reach out for additional information.

Step 4

### Corrective Action.

Napster will take action as warranted based on the outcome of the investigation.
