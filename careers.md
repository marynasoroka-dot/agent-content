Who we are

## A 25 year-old startup.

Napster pioneered peer-to-peer music sharing before the industry had words for it.

Now we're doing it again — building AI-native products that put intelligent, human-feeling experiences into physical spaces, mobile platforms, and enterprise deployments around the world.  

Our products span AI agent technology, enterprise kiosks, holographic display, music creation, and spatial computing. Step 1

### Ship it, then improve it.

We believe in getting real things into the real world. We'd rather learn from a live deployment than spend another month in a planning doc. Speed is a skill we practice deliberately — and we hold quality accountable once something is out in the world

Step 2

### Own the outcome.

There are no handoffs that excuse you from caring about the result. Engineers care about the user experience. Product managers care about the code. Operations cares about everything. We hire people who feel ownership across boundaries, not just within them.

Step 3

### Build with integrity.

AI is still being defined — and the choices we make now matter. We take our responsibility to users seriously: we're transparent about what our products do, thoughtful about safety and privacy, and honest when we get something wrong. That's not compliance; it's culture.
