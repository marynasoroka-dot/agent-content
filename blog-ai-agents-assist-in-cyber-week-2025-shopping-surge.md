# AI agents assist in Cyber Week 2025 shopping surge

Traffic from AI-powered tools to retail sites surged 670% on Cyber Monday alone, and traffic is up 760% overall from Nov. 1 to Dec. 1.

Napster

Cyber Week 2025 broke records and established a new standard for online retail. According to Adobe Analytics, Cyber Monday sales hit an all-time high of $14.25 billion, contributing to a massive $44.2 billion for the five-day period.

Deep discounts on electronics and apparel drove volume, but a significant shift occurred in consumer behavior. Artificial intelligence emerged as a primary driver of the season's success rather than a novelty. Traffic from AI-powered tools to retail sites surged 670% on Cyber Monday alone, and traffic is up 760% overall from Nov. 1 to Dec. 1.

This is the second year in a row that AI has made a meaningful impact on holiday shopping, according to Adobe’s research. And it’s clear why: Agentic AI has become more sophisticated, with the ability to compare products, find deals, and make recommendations that lead to higher conversion rates and deeper engagement than traditional traffic sources.

This success comes at a critical time, because the industry still battles persistent operational friction points. Despite the revenue records, the structural challenges of e-commerce remain costly. Typical online transaction completion rates still hover around a meager 2.5% to 3%, according to Napster research. Approximately 70% of digital shopping carts are abandoned before checkout. And roughly 18% of online purchases, or an estimated $247 billion annually, are returned.

## **AI Companions Can Empower Consumers and Retailers**

Forward-thinking tech companies are moving beyond chatbots to solve these headaches. The **Napster Holiday Gift Guide**, which debuted this season, is an example of what this evolution could look like.

Napster uses its Napster Spaces technology to replace static product catalogs with interactive 3D environments. Shoppers enter a branded digital space staffed by AI Companions instead of scrolling through static webpages. These agents act as expert sales associates that are knowledgeable about the brand's inventory. They can guide the journey from discovery to checkout and offer specific advice on inquiries such as sizing to reduce return rates.

Agentic AI tools attack the root causes of cart abandonment and returns by providing this layer of human-like expertise. Customers can buy with more confidence and are more likely to come away with a satisfying shopping experience when AI helps guide the experience. A custom shopper journey, with a context-rich, hyperpersonalized touch, simply isn’t possible at scale without this technology – and consumers are coming to expect that level of care.

The record-breaking numbers of 2025 prove that consumers are ready to spend, but they demand a smarter and more supportive experience. The winning retailers in 2026 will use AI to make shopping effortless.
